TENANCY AGREEMENT
(Executed under the Uttar Pradesh Regulation of Urban Premises Tenancy Act, 2021)

This Tenancy Agreement ("Agreement") is made and executed at Lucknow on this 15th day of
May, 2026,

BETWEEN

Mr. Vinod Kumar Srivastava, aged about 61 years, resident of 14/2, Hazratganj, Lucknow –
226001, Uttar Pradesh (hereinafter referred to as the "LANDLORD"),

AND

Mr. Abhishek Tiwari, aged about 29 years, presently residing at Flat No. 4B, Shakti
Residency, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow – 226010, Uttar Pradesh (hereinafter referred to as the
"TENANT").

WHEREAS the Landlord is the lawful owner of the residential flat bearing No. 4B, Shakti
Residency, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow – 226010, admeasuring approximately 950 sq. ft. built-up
area ("the Premises"), and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following
terms and conditions, in accordance with the Uttar Pradesh Regulation of Urban Premises
Tenancy Act, 2021 ("the Act"):

Section 4 — TENANCY AGREEMENT
1. Period of Tenancy: The tenancy shall be for a period of 11 (eleven) months
   commencing from 1st June, 2026, renewable thereafter by mutual written consent.
2. Rent: The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of ₹18,500/- (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Five
   Hundred only), payable in advance on or before the 7th day of each calendar month.
3. Security Deposit: The Tenant has paid to the Landlord a sum of ₹37,000/- (Rupees
   Thirty-Seven Thousand only), being two months' rent, as an interest-free refundable
   security deposit, in accordance with the limits contemplated under the Act.
4. Revision of Rent: The rent payable shall stand revised upon renewal of the tenancy,
   as may be mutually agreed in writing between the parties and, failing agreement, as
   determined by the Rent Authority under Section 9 of the Act.
5. Purpose: The Premises shall be used by the Tenant solely for residential purposes.

Section 11 — SECURITY DEPOSIT AND REFUND
The security deposit referred to above shall be refunded by the Landlord to the Tenant
at the time of the Tenant vacating the Premises, after making deductions, if any, for
liability of the Tenant on account of rent or damage caused to the Premises, if any,
other than normal wear and tear. Where the Landlord fails to refund the deposit within
the period specified under the Rules made under the Act, the Landlord shall be liable to
pay interest on the amount withheld, at the rate prescribed by the Rent Authority.

Section 15 — REPAIR AND MAINTENANCE OF PROPERTY
Unless otherwise agreed, the Landlord shall be responsible for repairs relating to
structural matters, external walls, roof and common plumbing, and the Tenant shall be
responsible for minor repairs and day-to-day maintenance of fixtures within the Premises.

Section 7 — RESTRICTION ON SUB-LETTING
The Tenant shall not sub-let the whole or any part of the Premises without the prior
written consent of the Landlord and, where required, without informing the Rent
Authority in the manner prescribed under the Act.

Section 21 — PROTECTION AGAINST EVICTION
The Tenant shall not be evicted from the Premises during the continuance of the tenancy
agreement except in accordance with the grounds and procedure prescribed under Section
21 of the Act.

TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon expiry of the term by giving the other
party one (1) calendar month's prior written notice, or as otherwise provided under the
Act.

GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the Uttar Pradesh Regulation of Urban Premises
Tenancy Act, 2021 and the Rules made thereunder, and disputes, if any, shall lie before
the Rent Authority / Rent Court having jurisdiction over Lucknow.
