LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Leave and License Agreement ("Agreement") is made and executed at Mumbai on this
28th day of March, 2026,

BETWEEN

Mr. Rajesh Kumar Deshmukh, aged about 54 years, residing at Flat No. 302, Sagar
Apartments, Andheri West, Mumbai – 400058 (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSOR",
which expression shall unless repugnant to the context mean and include his heirs,
executors, administrators and assigns) of the ONE PART,

AND

Ms. Priya Suresh Nair, aged about 27 years, residing at present at Flat No. 302, Sagar
Apartments, Andheri West, Mumbai – 400058 (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSEE",
which expression shall unless repugnant to the context mean and include her heirs,
executors, administrators and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of the residential flat bearing No. 302,
admeasuring approximately 620 sq. ft. carpet area, situated on the 3rd floor of Sagar
Apartments, Andheri West, Mumbai – 400058 ("the said Premises"), and is desirous of
granting leave and license in respect of the said Premises to the Licensee purely on a
leave and license basis and not by way of any lease or tenancy;

AND WHEREAS the Licensee has approached the Licensor for grant of such leave and
license and the Licensor has agreed to grant the same on the terms and conditions
hereinafter appearing;

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AND IT IS HEREBY AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS
FOLLOWS:

Clause 1. GRANT OF LICENSE
The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee leave and license to use and occupy the said
Premises purely as a licensee and not as a tenant, for residential purposes only.

Clause 2. TERM
This license shall be for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st April, 2026
and ending on 28th February, 2027, unless renewed or extended by mutual written consent
of both parties.

Clause 3. LICENSE FEE
The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly license fee (compensation) of
₹28,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Eight Thousand only), payable in advance on or before the 5th
day of each English calendar month, by bank transfer to the account designated by the
Licensor.

Clause 4. LATE PAYMENT
In the event the Licensee fails to pay the license fee by the 5th of the month, the
Licensor may charge a late fee not exceeding ₹500/- per week of delay.

Clause 5. USE OF PREMISES
The Licensee shall use the said Premises for residential purposes only and shall not use
the same for any illegal, immoral or commercial purpose, and shall not create any
nuisance to neighbouring occupants.

Clause 6. ENHANCEMENT OF LICENSE FEE
In the event this Agreement is renewed beyond the initial term, the license fee payable
for the renewed term shall stand enhanced by 10% (ten percent) per annum over the
license fee payable in the immediately preceding term.

Clause 7. SUB-LETTING
The Licensee shall not sub-let, assign or part with possession of the said Premises or
any part thereof to any third party under any circumstances.

Clause 8. ALTERATIONS
The Licensee shall not make any structural alteration, addition or change to the said
Premises without the prior written consent of the Licensor.

Clause 9. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Licensee has paid to the Licensor, on execution of this Agreement, a sum of
₹1,50,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand only) as an interest-free refundable
security deposit. The said deposit shall be refunded by the Licensor to the Licensee
within 30 (thirty) days of the Licensee vacating and handing over vacant possession of
the said Premises, after deducting therefrom any arrears of license fee, unpaid utility
charges, and the cost of repairing any damage to the said Premises beyond normal wear
and tear.

Clause 10. UTILITIES
The Licensee shall bear and pay, directly to the concerned authorities, all charges
towards electricity, water and cooking gas consumed on the said Premises during the
subsistence of this Agreement.

Clause 11. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE
Minor repairs and day-to-day upkeep of the said Premises shall be the responsibility of
and at the cost of the Licensee. Major structural repairs, including to the roof,
external walls, plumbing risers and the building's common areas, shall remain the
responsibility of the Licensor.

Clause 12. INSPECTION
The Licensor or his authorised representative shall be entitled to inspect the said
Premises at reasonable times upon giving the Licensee at least 48 hours' prior notice.

Clause 13. SOCIETY MAINTENANCE CHARGES
The monthly society maintenance charges levied by the housing society in respect of the
said Premises shall be borne by the Licensor.

Clause 14. TERMINATION AND NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement prior to expiry of the term by giving the
other party one (1) calendar month's notice in writing. Upon expiry of such notice, the
Licensee shall vacate and hand over peaceful vacant possession of the said Premises to
the Licensor.

Clause 15. NO TENANCY CREATED
It is expressly agreed and understood between the parties that nothing contained in this
Agreement shall be construed as creating any tenancy, sub-tenancy or any right, title or
interest whatsoever in favour of the Licensee in respect of the said Premises, other than
the personal permissive right to occupy the same during the subsistence of this
Agreement, in accordance with Section 24 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999.

Clause 16. REGISTRATION
This Agreement shall be registered by the parties in accordance with Section 55 of the
Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999, and the stamp duty and registration charges shall be
borne equally by both parties.

Clause 17. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Mumbai,
Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising herefrom.
