This Lease Agreement is made in Maharashtra for a residential flat.

The Landlord and the Tenant agree to the terms below for the property in Andheri West, Mumbai, Maharashtra.

The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of ₹1,50,000 (INR One Lakh Fifty Thousand only) at the time of signing.

3. Dispute history
The Tenant previously filed an FIR and a police complaint concerning alleged criminal intimidation related to the premises. An ongoing suit and a court order are referenced in prior correspondence. This clause exists only for product testing of the Escalation Guard.

4. Lease start
The lease commencing from 1 Apr 2026 and shall continue subject to the terms herein.

5. Lease end
The lease expires on 31 Mar 2027 unless renewed in writing.

6. Notice
Either party may terminate this agreement by giving 1 month prior notice in writing.

7. Rent
Monthly rent of ₹45,000 is payable on or before the 5th of each calendar month.

8. Use
The premises shall be used solely for residential purposes as a 1BHK flat.
