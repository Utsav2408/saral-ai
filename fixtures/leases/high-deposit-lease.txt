RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT — HIGH DEPOSIT FIXTURE

This Lease Agreement is made in Maharashtra for a residential flat.
Deposit is intentionally high versus monthly rent to exercise conflict rules.

1. Parties
The Landlord and the Tenant agree to the terms below for the property in Pune, Maharashtra.

2. Deposit
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of ₹5,00,000 (INR Five Lakh only) at the time of signing.

3. Lease start
The lease commencing from 1 May 2026.

4. Lease end
The lease expires on 30 Apr 2027 unless renewed in writing.

5. Notice
Either party may terminate this agreement by giving 1 month prior notice in writing.

6. Rent
Monthly rent of ₹40,000 is payable on or before the 5th of each calendar month.

7. Use
The premises shall be used solely for residential purposes as a 2BHK flat.

8. Maintenance
The Tenant shall keep the premises in good condition and report repairs promptly.
